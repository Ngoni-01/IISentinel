# IISentinel — The Pitch (one shot, use verbatim where marked)

## The line (memorize — say it in the first 20 seconds and again at the end)
"The failure warnings Western plants pay millions for — at $80 a sensor,
built for African networks."

## The 3-minute demo script
1. OPEN on the living map (Sentinel X home, projector mode ON if the room is bright).
   "Every asset, one pane of glass. Green is money being made."
2. KILL a pump (Cascade Studio → Mining pump cascade, or shift+click in classic).
   Say NOTHING while it propagates. Let the red spread do the talking.
3. HOLD UP THE PHONE when the SMS arrives. "That alert just reached a shift
   supervisor's pocket. No app. No smartphone required. 2G is enough."
   ← This is the moment. Everything else is garnish.
4. ONE CLICK into the drawer. "And unlike a black box, it shows its work —
   here's exactly why the score fell." (XAI factor breakdown)
5. STOP. Take questions. Cascade Studio full-storm only if asked.

## The CBS sentence (say BEFORE any blasting demo, unprompted, verbatim)
"IISentinel is advisory early-warning — read-only, no control path,
subordinate to certified blasting systems. It adds eyes, never hands."

## Positioning (mining-first)
- Beachhead: Mining OT condition monitoring. Highest downtime cost, worst
  tooling, aging equipment with zero telemetry, safety narrative.
- Expansion (the edition system IS the roadmap): tower power monitoring for
  telecoms (generator runtime, battery state, FUEL THEFT — the bleeding wound
  at thousands of sites), then network edition, then ESG/compliance reporting.
- Ten-year moat: anonymized cross-site failure data across African industry.
  Software is copyable in months; that dataset never will be.

## Pricing anchor (put a number on paper — adjust, don't remove)
| Item | Anchor |
|---|---|
| Mining OT Edition | $8–15 / asset / month (per-shaft bundles negotiable) |
| Pi sensor node | ~$120 installed (hardware at cost + fitting) |
| Deployment & training | one-off, per site |
| Pilot (30 days, ≤10 assets, 2 Pi nodes) | ~$500 or free against a signed LOI |
Anchor sentence: "One avoided hour of pump downtime pays for the platform for a year."

## Objection answers (they WILL ask)
- "Why not Zabbix/PRTG?" → "Those monitor IT. This monitors a pump with no
  network card, over a link that drops daily, and texts a supervisor on 2G.
  Different problem, different design."
- "Is the AI accurate?" → "It learns your plant's normal and flags deviation
  days before static thresholds trip. Day one it's auditable rules — you can
  read the formula. And we've defined the pilot metrics up front: alert
  precision, lead-time-to-failure, false positives per device-week."
- "Who answers at 2am?" → "Deployment and first-line support through local
  systems-integrator partners; we're the platform layer." 
- "Security for OT?" → "Outbound-only, read-only architecture — the platform
  physically cannot send a command to plant equipment. IEC 62443 alignment is
  on the roadmap."
- "What if you get hired / leave?" → the SI partner model again, plus: "the
  pilot contract includes source escrow if that unlocks the deal."

## Pilot success metrics (agree IN WRITING before day 1)
- Alert precision ≥ 70% (alerts that a technician confirms were real)
- ≥ 1 degradation flagged before the shift crew noticed it
- False positives < 2 per device-week after week 1 calibration
- Weekly PDF report lands in the engineering manager's inbox automatically

## The ask (end with this, then stop talking)
"One 30-day pilot site — ten assets, one shaft — and one letter of intent.
That's the whole ask."
